# Original downloadable-pack sources

Two collections contain 24 newly authored `.milk` presets across eight visual
families: polar fields, rings, tiled geometry, orbital curves, color waves,
ribbons, volume noise and blurred fields. Every preset carries per-file CC0
attribution in its pack manifest; there are no external texture dependencies.
These are additional original works, not relicensed historical community packs.

`tools/build_original_pack_candidates.py` packages these directories on the
private JIT runner and produces an exact inventory for qualification. This source
change does not generate archives or invent a rendering/rights-review report.
The signed catalog continues to require the full existing qualification policy
before any broader pack becomes downloadable. Keep candidate archives outside
the public catalog until reviewed reports refer to their exact bytes.
